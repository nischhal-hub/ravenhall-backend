RAVENHALL - FEATURE: POS (POINT OF SALE) FOR IN-PERSON BOOKINGS
===============================================================

WHAT IT IS
A staff screen at the front desk for customers who walk in. Staff pick the
customer, pick lanes and times, apply a discount if needed, take payment
(cash, card, etc.) and print a receipt - without the customer using the website.


WHO CAN USE IT
STAFF and ADMIN. All /api/pos routes require login and one of those roles.
Bookings made here are tagged source = POS and bookedById = the staff member,
so reports can separate them from online bookings.


THE FLOW
1. Find or add the customer
     - search existing customers (name/email/phone)
     - or add a walk-in customer (first name, last name, optional email/phone)
     - or continue as "Guest" (no details)
2. View the lane/slot grid for a date and choose one or more slots
3. Price check (calculate-price) - shows the discount breakdown
4. Create the booking with a payment method
     - paid now  -> booking CONFIRMED, payment SUCCEEDED
     - pay later -> booking PENDING, payment PENDING; collect later via /pay
5. Show / print the receipt


API (all under /api/pos)
  GET   /customers?search=         search customers
  POST  /customers                 create walk-in customer
  GET   /customers/:id             customer detail
  GET   /lanes-and-slots?date=YYYY-MM-DD[&laneId=]   availability grid
  POST  /calculate-price           price + discount breakdown
  POST  /bookings                  create booking (+ payment record)
  GET   /bookings                  list POS bookings (filters)
  GET   /bookings/:id              one booking
  GET   /bookings/:id/receipt      receipt data
  POST  /bookings/:id/pay          collect payment on a pending booking

Create booking body (main fields)
  slotIds[]  (or slotId)          required
  customerId | customer{...} | isGuest:true     one of these required
  paymentMethod                   CASH | CARD | STRIPE | BANK_TRANSFER | OTHER
  paymentStatus                   SUCCEEDED (default) | PENDING
  discountCode, customDiscountPct (0-100), customDiscountAmount, discountReason
  notes


CUSTOMER HANDLING
- Existing customerId ............ used as is.
- New customer details ........... if the email already exists, that account is
                                   reused (phone added if missing); otherwise a
                                   walk-in account is created.
- Guest .......................... a throwaway account "Walk-in Guest" with an
                                   address like guest_<time>@guest.ravenhall.com
                                   and a random password.
No confirmation email is sent to guest addresses.


PRICING RULES (src/modules/pos/pos.service.ts -> calculatePrice)
- Lane hire = sum of each slot's lane hourly rate.
- A flat $10 equipment fee (src/utils/pricing.ts) is added once per booking -
  the same as online bookings. Discounts never apply to the fee.
- Percentage discount = the HIGHEST of: member plan %, discount-code %, staff
  custom %, applied to lane hire. They do not add together.
- Then an optional flat staff discount (customDiscountAmount) is taken off lane
  hire.
- Total discount can never exceed lane hire; final amount never below the fee.
- calculate-price returns laneHire, equipmentFee, totalAmount (= both),
  discountBreakdown and finalAmount. The receipt shows lane hire and equipment
  fee separately (equipmentFee is 0 on older bookings made before the fee).
- Membership is looked up for the chosen customer (active, not expired).
- Discount codes are checked for active flag, valid dates and max uses; the
  code's usedCount goes up when a booking uses it.
- discountReason is saved into the booking notes with "POS Booking [METHOD]".


SAFETY
- Slots on the same lane and day must be back-to-back (no gaps), otherwise 400.
  Different lanes or days in one booking are allowed (e.g. a group taking two
  lanes).
- Slots are claimed with ONE conditional update inside the database
  transaction (only still-free slots match), so two staff booking the same slot
  at the same instant cannot both succeed - the loser gets 409.
- Cancelled bookings cannot be paid; already-paid confirmed bookings cannot be
  paid twice.
- A confirmation email is sent after payment (not for guests); an email
  failure never fails the booking.
